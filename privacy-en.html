Mei Hua Zhao Hua · Privacy Policy

没话找话 · Effective November 15, 2026
One-sentence summary: this app does not collect any data.
No accounts. No servers. No network requests. No third-party SDKs. No ads. No analytics.
The daily prompt you see is selected locally on your device from a built-in prompt library, shown in rotation.
All functionality runs entirely on your device.

1. What We Collect

Nothing.

This app does not transmit any data to any external server and does not store any data externally. Which prompt to show today is determined locally on your device from your calendar and a built-in prompt pool; all computation happens on your device.

2. Permissions We Request

None.

This app does not request any device permissions. It does not access contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, notifications, local network, or the advertising identifier (IDFA).

3. Third Parties

None.

This app does not embed any third-party SDK, ad network, or analytics tool. This includes, but is not limited to: Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, Umeng, Sentry, or any advertising SDK.

4. Children's Privacy

Because no data is collected, there is no "children's data collection" issue. This app is intended for family use; minors may use it with parental guidance.

5. Data Deletion

Because no user data is stored, there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of its content (the built-in prompt library is removed along with the app).

6. Policy Changes

If this app adds any networked functionality in the future, this policy will be updated and resubmitted with the next app version for App Store review. This page will be updated at the same time, with the effective date shown at the top of the page.
