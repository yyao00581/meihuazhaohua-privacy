Privacy Policy · Mei Hua Zhao Hua

没话找话 · Effective November 15, 2026
This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no developer-operated servers, no networking code, and no third-party SDKs, advertising components, or analytics tools. The daily content is distributed as a bundled resource within the app, and all functionality runs entirely on your device. This policy is prepared in accordance with App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(i) and describes the app's data practices and the rights available to you.

1. Data Collection

This app collects no data. Specifically, the app:

  • does not send data to any server and performs no network communication of any kind;
  • does not store data in any external location, and performs no caching or backup;
  • does not collect device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or any device fingerprint;
  • does not collect user input, usage history, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any other information.

The daily content shown is determined solely by your device's local date and the bundled prompt library distributed with the app. This computation occurs entirely on your device and involves no data upload.

2. Device Permissions

This app requests no device permissions whatsoever. Specifically, it does not request location, photo library, camera, microphone, contacts, notification, local network, or advertising tracking permissions.

3. Third-Party Services

This app does not share user data with any third party, and there is no third party with access to user data. The app does not embed any third-party SDK, advertising network, or analytics tool, including but not limited to Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, Umeng, Sentry, and any advertising SDK.

4. Your Rights

Because this app does not collect or store any personal data, your rights are exercised as follows:

Right Status under this app
Right to be informed This policy constitutes full disclosure. No undisclosed data processing activities exist.
Right of access and portability The app holds no personal data attributable to any individual user, so no user data is accessible or exportable.
Right to rectification The app holds no user data, so there is no information requiring correction.
Right to erasure The app stores no user data and therefore has no data to delete. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of its content, including the bundled prompt library.
Right to withdraw consent No data is collected, so no consent-based processing authorization has been obtained or relied upon. There is no consent requiring withdrawal.
Complaints and reports If you believe the app's practices infringe your rights, contact us at the email address in Section 8. We will respond within fifteen business days.

5. Data Retention

This app stores no user data and therefore has no data retention period. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of its content.

6. Children's Privacy

Because no data is collected, the collection of children's personal information does not apply. This app is designed for family use; minors may use it under parental guidance.

7. Policy Updates

The current version of this policy is submitted with app version 1.0. If a future version adds any networked functionality, we will update this policy first and submit the updated version for App Store review. The effective date of any update will be displayed at the top of this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or complaints regarding this policy or the app's data practices, please contact us at:
