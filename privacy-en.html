Privacy Policy · Mei Hua Zhao Hua

没话找话 · Effective November 15, 2026
This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no developer-operated servers, no networking code, and no third-party SDKs, advertising components, or analytics tools. The topic library and its preset pairings are distributed as a bundled resource within the app, and all functionality runs entirely on your device. The app saves a small amount of usage state on your device only (which topics were shown today, which one you selected, and the IDs of topics you have already chosen) so that topics are not repeated. This state contains no information that could identify you, is never uploaded, synced, or shared, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. This policy is prepared in accordance with App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(i) and describes the app's data practices and the rights available to you.

1. Data Collection

This app collects no personal data. Specifically, the app:

  • does not send data to any server and performs no network communication of any kind;
  • does not collect device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or any device fingerprint;
  • does not collect user input, usage history, location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any other information;
  • does not store, cache, or back up any data outside your device.

Which topics are shown is determined by your device's local date, the bundled topic library, and the local usage state described in Section 2. Each day the app shows one or two topics, which may be related, opposite in meaning, or entirely unrelated; once you select and confirm one, it will not appear again. This computation occurs entirely on your device and involves no data upload.

2. Usage State Kept on Your Device

To keep the same topics stable throughout the day and to ensure that topics you have already chosen do not reappear, the app stores the following state inside its app sandbox on your device:

Stored item Purpose
Current date (e.g. 2026-11-15) Detects a new day so that topics are dealt again
IDs of the topics shown today (one or two) Keeps the same set of topics if you reopen the app the same day
ID of the topic you confirmed today Shows the "selected" state and prevents duplicate confirmation
List of topic IDs already chosen Prevents chosen topics from appearing again; clears automatically once the whole library has been used

All of the above consists solely of dates and topic IDs. It contains no name, account, device identifier, location, or any information that could identify you. It is stored only in the app's sandbox on your device, is never uploaded or synced, and is not shared with any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes it together with the app.

3. Device Permissions

This app requests no device permissions whatsoever. Specifically, it does not request location, photo library, camera, microphone, contacts, notification, local network, or advertising tracking permissions.

4. Third-Party Services

This app does not share user data with any third party, and there is no third party with access to user data. The app does not embed any third-party SDK, advertising network, or analytics tool, including but not limited to Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, Umeng, Sentry, and any advertising SDK.

5. Your Rights

Because this app collects no personal data and keeps on your device only the dates and topic IDs described above, your rights are exercised as follows:

Right Status under this app
Right to be informed This policy constitutes full disclosure, including the on-device state listed in Section 2. No undisclosed data processing activities exist.
Right of access and portability The on-device state is held by the app itself on your device. It belongs to no server-side account, and there is no user data that can be exported or migrated.
Right to rectification The on-device state consists only of dates and topic IDs and involves no personal information requiring correction.
Right to erasure Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its on-device content, including the usage state and the bundled topic library. The list of already-chosen topics also clears and resets automatically once the whole library has been used.
Right to withdraw consent No data is collected, so no consent-based processing authorization has been obtained or relied upon. There is no consent requiring withdrawal.
Complaints and reports If you believe the app's practices infringe your rights, contact us at the email address in Section 9. We will respond within fifteen business days.

6. Data Retention

The on-device usage state remains on your device until you uninstall the app, or until it clears and resets automatically after the whole topic library has been used. Beyond this, the app retains no data, and there is no server-side retention period.

7. Children's Privacy

Because no data is collected, the collection of children's personal information does not apply. This app is designed for family use; minors may use it under parental guidance.

8. Policy Updates

The current version of this policy is submitted with app version 1.0. If a future version adds any networked functionality, we will update this policy first and submit the updated version for App Store review. The effective date of any update will be displayed at the top of this page.
